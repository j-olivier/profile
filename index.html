resize-app

Mobile app

Put your pages and clips in the order you want, shrink them to the size you need, and share the result. Your files stay on your device.

  • Audio, joined & compressedImport or record clips, play them in the list, then join them into one small MP3.
  • PDFs & photos, rearrangedReorder and rotate pages, move them between files, read them full screen.
  • Simple gesturesPress and hold to reorder, swipe to delete or rotate, double-tap to zoom.

quickjs-pal

Open-source library

A version of Fabrice Bellard's QuickJS JavaScript engine that runs on any platform, even one without an operating system. To bring it to a new system, you write one small layer and leave the engine untouched.

QuickJS enginethe same code on every system
Platform layermemory, time, threads, locks and logging, in one place
Your systema console, a device, bare metal, or Windows and Unix by default
  • The host stays in controlFatal errors are reported to your program instead of shutting it down.
  • Builds in Visual StudioReady-made solution for 64-bit and 32-bit Windows.
